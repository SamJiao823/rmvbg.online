Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Our Core Promise

RMVBG is built on a simple principle: your images are yours, and yours alone. We never see them, never store them, never share them.

How Image Processing Works

All background removal happens entirely in your browser using client-side AI technology. Your images are never uploaded to any server. The AI model runs locally on your device.

  • No image data leaves your device
  • No server receives, stores, or processes your images
  • Images are automatically cleared when you close the browser tab

Data We Collect

We use standard analytics services to understand how visitors use our website:

  • Google Analytics (GA4): Collects anonymized page view data, device type, browser type, and approximate geographic location. No personally identifiable information.
  • Microsoft Clarity: Collects anonymized session recordings and heatmaps to improve user experience. No personal data.

You can opt out of analytics by using browser privacy settings or ad blockers.

Cookies

  • Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity use cookies for session tracking
  • Google AdSense uses cookies to serve relevant ads
  • No login cookies — we have no user accounts
  • You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdSense: Displays advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. Learn more.
  • jsDelivr CDN: The AI model is loaded from jsDelivr's CDN for performance. jsDelivr does not receive your images.
  • MailerLite: Used for optional newsletter subscriptions. Only your email address is stored if you choose to subscribe.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
