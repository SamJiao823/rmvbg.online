Advanced Tips for Better Results

Tip 1: Use a plain, contrasting background when taking photos. A white wall behind a person = perfect removal every time.
Tip 2: For ecommerce, photograph products on a slightly textured surface (not pure white) — this helps the AI distinguish product from background better.
Tip 3: If the result has rough edges, try increasing your original photo's contrast before uploading. The AI works best with clear foreground/background separation.
