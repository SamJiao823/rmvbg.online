Frequently Asked Questions

Is this background remover really free? ▼
Yes, completely free! No hidden fees, no credit card required. You can remove backgrounds from unlimited images without paying a cent. We make money from optional ads.
Can I use the downloaded images for commercial purposes? ▼
Yes! All images you download are free for commercial use. No attribution required. Use them for your business, products, marketing materials, social media, or any other purpose.
Are my images private and secure? ▼
100% private. Your images are processed entirely in your browser using AI. They never get uploaded to any server, never get stored, and never leave your device. This is a key advantage over other background removers that require server uploads.
What image formats can I download? ▼
You can download your image in two formats: PNG (transparent background) — perfect for overlaying on other images or designs, and JPG (white background) — ideal for product photos and documents.
Is there a file size or resolution limit? ▼
The maximum file size is 10 MB. We support JPEG, PNG, and WebP formats. For best results, use images with clear subject-background contrast.
Why is it slow the first time? ▼
The first time you use the tool, it needs to download the AI model to your browser (about 40MB). This is a one-time download — subsequent uses will be much faster since the model is cached in your browser.
Do you store my images on your server? ▼
No, never. We don't have any server storage for user images. Everything happens in your browser. Once you close the page, all data is gone. Your privacy is our top priority.
